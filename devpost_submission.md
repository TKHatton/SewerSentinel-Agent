# SewerSentinel - Devpost Submission

## Text Description (~200 words) - For Submission Form

SewerSentinel transforms reactive sewer maintenance into proactive infrastructure protection using Gemini 3's advanced multimodal capabilities.

**The Problem:** 850 billion gallons of untreated sewage discharge into U.S. waterways annually due to pipe failures. Current inspection requires humans to watch thousands of hours of footage—often missing subtle degradation until it's too late.

**The Solution:** SewerSentinel is an autonomous marathon agent that doesn't just detect defects—it predicts failures and prioritizes repairs.

**Gemini 3 Integration:**
- **1M Token Context Window:** Analyzes entire pipe inspection histories for cross-temporal patterns
- **Multimodal Video Understanding:** Processes CCTV footage with spatial-temporal reasoning
- **Thought Signatures:** Tracks evolving pipe conditions across multiple inspections
- **Thinking Levels:** Deep causal reasoning about degradation factors (traffic load, soil conditions, weather)
- **Tool Calling:** Integrates external data sources for contextual analysis

**Key Features:**
- PACP-compliant defect classification (16+ categories)
- Failure timeline prediction with confidence intervals
- Risk-based repair prioritization
- Cost-benefit analysis showing proactive vs. emergency repair savings
- Executive dashboard for city infrastructure managers

SewerSentinel runs continuously as an autonomous agent—the "marathon agent" approach Google DeepMind envisioned for the Action Era.

**Impact:** Prevents sinkholes, protects public health, and saves cities millions in emergency repairs.
